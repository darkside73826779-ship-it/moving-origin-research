# ADVISOR PROPOSAL v1 — L8 Instantiation (Selective-Risk Homeostat)
**Chain document 2 of 6** · Source: advisor session (Claude family), 2026-08-19, responding to the L8 Advisor Consultation Package (document 1). Transcribed verbatim from the session record; headers added for the review chain.

---

**Recommendation in one paragraph.** Instantiate the homeostatic variable as **windowed selective risk** — the error rate on queries the system chooses to answer — regulated by the abstention machinery already built for L10, with the L7 mirror's calibrated confidence as the control signal. This is the one candidate that satisfies all five criteria without bolting anything synthetic onto the system, and it is the faithful operationalization of the Damasio/Seth lineage the law itself cites: interoceptive signals exist *because* regulation requires them, so corrupt the interoception and regulation fails even when the world is fine. Concretely: the system's realized risk on answered queries must stay at or below a pre-registered target R*; the mechanism that keeps it there is the mirror-driven abstention decision; degrade the mirror's calibration and wrong abstention decisions push realized risk above target dose-dependently. The regulation loop is not an added controller — the mirror plus the threshold *is* the controller, already load-bearing in the architecture.

**Q1 — why this variable and not the spec's other examples.** A resource level (latency, compute) fails criterion 4: nothing about regulating latency depends on mirror calibration, so L8 would be untestable or vacuously false. A calibration metric fails criterion 3 in the fatal direction — if the homeostatic variable *is* ECE, then injecting calibration noise raises it by construction, and L8 becomes a tautology that a decorative-stakes system passes. An error budget is essentially selective risk under another name, so the recommendation is the rigorous version of that category. Index-staleness (re-resolution debt) is a genuine fourth option native to the moving-origin mechanism, but its coupling to the mirror would have to be *designed in*, which drifts toward building the variable to pass the test — the exact failure L8 exists to catch.

**Q2 — target and regulation-error definition.** Set R* from oracle-grounded calibration, candidate-blind per Ruling 9: the selective risk achievable by a well-calibrated reference at a pre-registered coverage point, plus a fixed margin. Define regulation error as integrated hinge deviation — the mean over query windows of max(0, r_w − R*), where r_w is realized risk in window w. Hinge rather than absolute distance, because being *under* target is healthy regulation, not error; integrated over windows rather than end-of-run, because regulation is a process property. With the 200-query battery, windows of 40–50 queries give four to five windows per seed — enough for a per-seed scalar, and the dose-response Spearman then runs over four dose levels per seed, where ρ ≥ 0.8 on four points means at most zero inversions: strict, but that's the locked bar working as intended. Pre-register one more thing the package doesn't mention: a **Level-0 baseline gate** — at zero noise the homeostat must be in band (regulation error ≈ 0). A dose-response measured from an already-violated baseline is meaningless, and without this gate a broken build could produce a beautiful monotone curve that certifies nothing.

**Q3 — noise mechanism.** Perturb the mirror's *output signal*, not its weights: additive Gaussian noise in logit space on the confidence estimates (or equivalently, mixing the confidence toward a seeded random draw with coefficient ε). Weight-space noise makes "dose" architecture-dependent and irreproducible across implementations; logit-space noise operationalizes "self-model calibration degraded" as literally and measurably as the law's text asks, degrades ECE smoothly and monotonically, and leaves answer content untouched — which matters, because it isolates the causal path L8 claims: self-signal corruption → regulation failure, with the world held fixed.

**Q4 — dose calibration, and a tension to resolve now.** Define doses in *design units* — σ as pre-registered multiples of the mirror's nominal logit variance, derived from oracle/synthetic profiles — and then report realized ΔECE per dose as a manipulation check, not a tuning input. The tension: the intuitive alternative, calibrating σ to hit target ΔECE values *on the candidate's mirror*, requires candidate outputs and therefore violates Ruling 9's candidate-blind requirement as written. Design-unit doses with a post-hoc realized-effect report stay blind, stay pre-registerable, and still let the JUDGE verify the manipulation actually manipulated. Flag this explicitly in the ARCHITECT's instantiation so it's a documented choice, not an accident.

**Q5 — specificity component.** Use the **memory store**, severity-matched, and treat this as the theoretically loaded contrast — it's Damasio's own distinction, world-mapping versus self-mapping. It's also the *harder* and therefore more valuable test, and here is the insight the whole design turns on: memory corruption raises task difficulty, but a well-calibrated mirror *detects* its own degraded competence and abstains more — so coverage drops while selective risk stays regulated. Regulation error stays flat not because nothing happened, but because regulation *worked*. Mirror corruption, by contrast, breaks the detector itself. Severity-matching should follow Ruling 3's standardized-proximal-effect approach: each memory dose matched to the mirror dose producing equal standardized effect on the component's own output (retrieval fidelity degradation matched to ΔECE in standardized units), with a pre-registered tolerance band. And the specificity claim should be strengthened from a single point to the **full dose curve**: pre-register that the memory-arm slope falls within a null band (|slope| below a fixed threshold with its CI) across all doses, not just that one dose fails to move the needle. Cross-component severity matching is the weakest joint in this entire test — a hostile reviewer will go there first — and full-curve specificity is the honest armor.

**Q6 — L14, including a contradiction to pre-empt.** The variable satisfies L14 with two build items: expose the windowed-risk register in the self-state vector the mirror reads (readability), and add next-window risk as a prediction target for the downstream consumer over recency-weighted features (predictive target for the thick present). "Affected by memory quality" is intrinsic — retrieval quality determines correctness. But notice the apparent contradiction: L14 requires the variable be *affected by memory quality*, while L8's specificity control requires memory perturbation *not raise regulation error*. These are compatible only under a distinction the spec must state explicitly: memory noise moves the **operating point** (coverage drops — the variable responds, satisfying L14) while **regulation error** stays controlled (risk holds at target, satisfying L8). Both facts come out of the same specificity-arm runs, which is elegant — but if the spec doesn't draw the distinction in writing, the program's own auditor will flag L8 and L14 as contradictory requirements, and it will be right to.

**Q7 — falsifiability.** The test can genuinely fail, in at least three honest ways: if the abstention threshold never actually binds — the mirror isn't load-bearing for the decision — mirror noise produces a flat curve and L8 fails, correctly detecting decorative stakes; if the system lacks calibrated abstention entirely, the memory arm raises selective risk and specificity fails; and monotone rise is *not* guaranteed by construction, because it requires the noise to actually flip abstention decisions near the threshold, which is an empirical property of the coupling, not an artifact of the design. The tautology route (regulation error defined as calibration error) is excluded by making the variable outcome-based. One residual to document honestly: L8 passing is *related to* L7 and L10 passing but not entailed by them — L7 says the mirror is calibrated, L10 says abstention behaves, L8 says the *link between them is load-bearing under graded degradation*. That's a distinct, falsifiable claim, and the spec should say so in one sentence to head off the "isn't this just L10 again" objection.

**Q8 — remaining risks.** Three. First, L8 and L10 now share machinery — pre-register whether they share runs and how the FWFP audit accounts for the shared evidence, or a multiplicity objection lands later. Second, per-seed aggregation needs exact pre-registration (all five seeds pass, or per-seed alpha in the M3 style) — the package cites the bars but not the aggregation rule, and that ambiguity is where post-hoc flexibility hides. Third, the standing one: this recommendation comes from inside the correlated stack. It should be attacked by the fresh-context CRITIC like any ARCHITECT proposal, and L8 specifically — the most philosophically loaded law in the constitution — is the strongest candidate for showing the external human reviewer *before* scoring rather than after, since a design flaw here found post-hoc costs fresh seeds that cannot be recovered.
