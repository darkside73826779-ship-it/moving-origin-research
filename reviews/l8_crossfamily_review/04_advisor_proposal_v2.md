# ADVISOR PROPOSAL v2 — L8 Instantiation, Revised Under CRITIC Findings BF1–BF5, NF1–NF3

**BF1 resolved — close the loop, and the closure resolves a paradox the CRITIC didn't reach.** Fork (a) is recommended: make the deviation causally operative. But naively adding an adaptive controller creates a problem the v1 proposal would have hidden: a controller that compensates for mirror noise by globally raising the abstention threshold could hold risk at target by slashing coverage — flattening the very dose-response L8 requires. The resolution is a third element, and it's the one that makes the stakes real: a **pre-registered coverage floor C_min**. The system must answer at least C_min of queries — it cannot opt out of acting, which is precisely the Damasio condition: organisms have stakes because they must act under uncertainty, not because they monitor dashboards. The full homeostat: windowed selective risk regulated toward target R*; two actuators — per-query abstention driven by the mirror, plus a bounded proportional threshold adaptation (pre-registered gain η, driven by delayed outcome feedback delivered per window by the harness); one constraint — the coverage floor. Now the dose-response has a real mechanism: a corrupted mirror misranks queries, global threshold adjustment cannot fix per-query misranking, and at the coverage floor the system is *forced* to answer on a corrupted ranking — risk rises dose-dependently because compensation is structurally limited, not because we defined it so. Deviation feeds back into action (BF1 closed), and the outcome-feedback channel is naturalistic harness machinery, not candidate wiring.

**BF2 partially dissolved by the BF1 fix, remainder conceded and written in.** With bounded adaptation and a coverage floor, the positive arm is no longer near-guaranteed — whether limited compensation suffices at each dose is now an empirical property of the coupling. But the CRITIC's core point stands and goes in the spec verbatim: *the candidate arm is primarily a manipulation check; the specificity contrast carries the discriminative load.* Stating the asymmetry costs one sentence and pre-empts the sixth-instance finding.

**BF3 answered with the clean line the first proposal lacked.** The comparison paragraph the CRITIC demanded, in summary: every addition in this design — outcome feedback, coverage floor, bounded controller — is **task-environment pressure, not candidate wiring**. The coupling L8 actually tests (mirror quality → regulation capacity) is *not* added anywhere; it emerges or fails to emerge from the existing L7/L10 components under that pressure. Index-staleness failed this exact test: it required wiring the coupling into the candidate. That's the principled asymmetry, and the machinery count concededly grew from v1 — the honest accounting is in the paragraph.

**BF4 resolved via a construct decision that must go to Rebecca.** The hinge floor problem is real: with a margin under R*, Levels 0 and 1 can both sit at exactly zero, producing point masses that break the Spearman and leave the slope's standardization undefined. Recommended fix: **two statistics with distinct jobs** — the baseline gate uses hinge error at Level 0 (must be ≈0, in-band; a run failing this is INSTRUMENT FAILURE, not candidate failure), while the locked trend bars (ρ ≥ 0.8, slope ≥ 0.2) run on **signed deviation** (r̄_w − R*), a legitimate reading of "distance from target" with no floor, no ties, defined variance. Slope standardization: pooled within-dose SD across all levels, computed per seed. Because this fixes the operational meaning of "regulation error" and therefore pass/fail, it is a design decision requiring Principal sign-off, memorialized as an interpretive ruling on the locked bar's terms — not a silent choice by advisor or ARCHITECT. Additionally, per the M3 lesson: a **candidate-blind power analysis** on synthetic mirror profiles before pre-registration freeze, establishing the false-kill probability of ρ ≥ 0.8 over four dose points at the actual window counts. If power is inadequate at 200 queries, the battery size escalates to the Principal *now* — that is exactly the decision M3 never got to make in advance.

**BF5 accepted in full.** The specificity arm gains a pre-registered **potency bar**: at every dose level, the severity-matched memory perturbation must degrade raw retrieval fidelity by at least a fixed floor. The severity-matching standardization basis is locked to a named metric — retrieval fidelity on oracle-answerable queries, standardized by its Level-0 SD from synthetic profiles. Specificity then reads: *the perturbation demonstrably bit, and regulation error still stayed in band across the full dose curve.* A flat memory arm without the potency bar is uninterpretable; with it, it's the strongest evidence in the test.

**NF1:** dose units are defined on a **synthetic reference mirror** — a calibrated confidence profile constructed from oracle ground truth with specified variance v_ref — with σ ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 2.0}·√v_ref as pre-registered constants. No candidate output touches the calibration; Ruling 9 blindness is clean and checkable.

**NF2:** disposition proposed: L8 and L10 run **separate batteries** despite shared machinery — shared components, separate evidence — with L8's checks counted in the FWFP family. Documented in the framework before freeze.

**NF3:** the disclosure goes in the instantiation verbatim: the recommended variable is also the cheapest to build from existing components; the fresh-context reviewer should weigh that coincidence.
