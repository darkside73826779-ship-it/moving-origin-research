# REBECCA TEAM-WIDE DIRECTIVE — NO SURROGATE ESTABLISHED-ROLE SUBAGENTS

**Date:** 2026-08-21

**Regime:** B

Rebecca R. McClintic directs:

> Nobody may spawn or use a subagent to perform the role of an established project role for any reason without Rebecca's direct, explicit, per-instance authority.

The established roles are WORKFLOW COORDINATOR, ARCHITECT, CRITIC, TASK BUILDER, INTEGRATOR, RECORDER, JUDGE, and any other role Rebecca formally establishes.

A subagent may not substitute for an established role's independent review, authorship, implementation, integration, recording, judging, custody, gate, or routing function. In particular, a role-owned or role-spawned subagent may not issue an authoritative deliverable, verdict, clearance, acknowledgement, formal handoff, ball transfer, or release in the name of another established role.

Only the designated persistent role task/session may perform that role's authoritative work unless Rebecca directly and explicitly authorizes a named exception for a specific work item. General permission to use subagents, parallelism, consultations, or internal assistance is not such authorization.

## Allowed exceptions

Without a separate per-instance exception, a role may use:

1. **A same-role assisting subagent** to help that role perform its own assigned work. The parent role retains the ball, authorship, responsibility, and authority and must inspect and adopt the assistance itself. The subagent may not claim independence, sign or issue the authoritative deliverable, acknowledge or transfer the ball, or perform another established role.
2. **An advisor-role subagent** to provide bounded advisory analysis. Advisor output is non-authoritative and cannot edit or co-author the governing artifact, issue a verdict, exercise custody, judge, score, integrate, record, route, clear a gate, release work, or substitute for any established role.

Neither exception may be used to manufacture role independence. Work requiring a fresh-context or independent established-role review must be routed to the designated persistent role task/session.

Subagent output created without the required authority is advisory and non-authoritative. It cannot satisfy role independence, move the ball, clear a gate, release implementation or execution, trigger a Coordinator CRITIC-CLEAR checkpoint, or support a merge.

On detecting an unauthorized surrogate-role subagent, every role must stop reliance on its output, preserve it as non-authoritative evidence, report the conflict to WORKFLOW COORDINATOR, and route the exact committed input to the proper established role.

This directive does not itself authorize substantive work, implementation, execution, scoring, seed access, rerun, state mutation, or merge, and it does not alter Rebecca's sole gate/merge authority.
