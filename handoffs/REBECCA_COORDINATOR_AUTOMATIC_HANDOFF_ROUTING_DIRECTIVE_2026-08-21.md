# REBECCA TEAM-WIDE DIRECTIVE — COORDINATOR-CENTERED AUTOMATIC HANDOFF ROUTING

**Date:** 2026-08-21

**Regime:** B

Rebecca R. McClintic directs that every established project role use the WORKFLOW COORDINATOR as the control room for formal handoff continuity.

## Required return path

After completing assigned work, remediation, review, implementation, integration, recording, or judging, the current owner must return one labeled FORMAL HANDOFF to WORKFLOW COORDINATOR with:

- timestamp;
- work item/gate;
- sender and intended next role;
- authoritative branch and routing-head SHA;
- substantive result/review SHA where distinct;
- artifact path;
- verdict/status;
- blockers and holds; and
- next expected event.

The role must stop after the return unless its existing authority explicitly permits continued same-role work. It may not bypass the Coordinator, infer a gate decision, release another role, merge, execute, score, or treat a direct recipient copy as authoritative routing.

## Coordinator behavior

WORKFLOW COORDINATOR shall:

1. verify the committed branch/SHA/artifact and role lineage;
2. reject surrogate-role or non-authoritative reviews;
3. update the local routing ledger;
4. automatically route the exact package to the next authorized persistent role without waiting for Rebecca to say “pass the ball”; and
5. obtain and record the receiving role's formal acknowledgement before treating ownership as transferred.

When the next decision belongs to Rebecca, the Coordinator shall stop automatic routing, retain custody, and present the exact decision package to Rebecca. No role may infer or speak for Rebecca's approval.

Rebecca may interrupt or redirect automatic routing at any time.

## Mandatory automatic-routing stops

Coordinator stops and escalates/presents rather than auto-routing on:

- Rebecca-only approval, release, gate, adoption, scoring, execution, or merge decisions;
- ambiguity or conflicting authoritative SHAs;
- missing committed/executable inputs;
- an unprescribed BLOCK return or material scope choice;
- protected-seed, courier, or scoring boundaries;
- requested merge, destructive action, external publication/action, or new authority requirement; and
- any constitutional, public-safety, role-independence, or standing STOP condition.

## Role independence and subagents

Only the designated persistent role task/session may satisfy an established role's authoritative gate. Same-role assisting and bounded advisor subagents remain subject to `handoffs/REBECCA_ESTABLISHED_ROLE_SUBAGENT_PROHIBITION_2026-08-21.md`; their output cannot independently move the ball or satisfy a fresh-context role review.

## Non-authorization

This directive changes routing continuity only. It does not authorize substantive work, implementation, training, execution, scoring, seed access/exposure, rerun, adoption, state/provenance mutation, public-status change, or merge.
