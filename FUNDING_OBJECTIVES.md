# Funding Objectives

**Project principal:** Rebecca R. McClintic

## Purpose

Funding would support independent validation and the next falsifiable stages of the program. It would not be used to relabel historical failures or tune locked results after observation.

## Priority uses

1. **Independent replication** — external reproduction of existing summaries and, where authorized, prospective experiments.
2. **Statistical and methodological review** — qualified review of stochastic-control design, multiplicity, calibration, and interpretation.
3. **Durable evidence hosting** — deposit of large raw artifacts, manifests, and checksums in a stable research-data repository with an accession identifier or DOI.
4. **Reproducible compute** — controlled environments, documented hardware, containerized non-scoring verification, and prospective execution infrastructure.
5. **Later milestones** — implementation and review of the remaining pre-specified temporal self-modeling and integration tests.
6. **Model-level experiments** — only after earlier gates justify them, evaluate the harness with larger models and adapter-based interventions.
7. **Qualified collaborators** — statistics, experimental design, machine learning, reproducibility engineering, and research governance.

## Deliverables suitable for a funding agreement

- versioned public protocols and pre-registrations;
- independent review reports;
- durable artifact deposits with checksums;
- automated non-scoring verification;
- prospective milestone reports that retain null and negative outcomes;
- a clear claims boundary separating temporal-mechanism evidence from consciousness or AGI claims.

## Current funding-stage proposition

The project has sufficient recorded evidence to justify a replication-and-validation phase. It has not completed the full research program, and M3 does not hold an overall pass. Funding should therefore be framed around testing and reducing uncertainty, not commercializing a proven consciousness technology.
