# AI Contribution and Human-Control Statement

AI systems contributed materially to this project. They assisted with:

- drafting experimental specifications and amendments;
- implementing Python harness and experiment code;
- generating tests and deterministic verification utilities;
- performing role-separated criticism and judgment;
- maintaining handoffs, state summaries, and provenance records.

The workflow separates proposal, implementation, criticism, judgment, recordkeeping, and execution roles to reduce—but not eliminate—correlated model error.

The human principal, Rebecca R. McClintic, retains authority over:

- research direction and milestone continuation;
- adoption or amendment of specifications;
- numerical bars, kill conditions, and scoring authorization;
- acceptance or rejection of role recommendations;
- merges to the governed historical record;
- public claims and funding requests.

AI-generated output is not treated as independent scientific validation merely because different role names or model sessions produced it. Claims of independent verification in this repository refer to the documented review procedure and recomputation performed, not to institutional or peer-reviewed independence unless explicitly stated.

Future funding priorities include independent human statistical review and external replication.
